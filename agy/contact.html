Emergency & Crisis Notice: We Are Not an Emergency Service

Mindful Space provides outpatient virtual counselling and is not a replacement for emergency care. The service is not intended for crisis intervention.

If you are in immediate danger or may harm yourself, contact your nearest hospital emergency department or local emergency services, and reach out to a trusted person who can stay with you.

National Emergency Helpline: Dial 112 across India (Police, Ambulance, Fire — please verify local availability in your district)
Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India): Toll-free 24/7 National Mental Health Helpline: 14416 or 1800-891-4416
KIRAN Helpline (MSJE, India): Toll-free 24/7 Mental Health Rehabilitation: 1800-599-0019
Vandrevala Foundation Helpline: 24/7 Free Mental Health Support: +91 9999 666 555

Immediate Emergency & Mental Health Resources in India

If you or someone you know is in acute distress or may harm themselves, please reach out to emergency resources immediately:

  • Hospital Emergency Department: Visit your nearest hospital emergency or casualty room.
  • National Emergency Number: Dial 112 (verify local availability in your region).
  • Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India): Call 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (24/7 toll-free).
  • KIRAN Helpline (MSJE, India): Call 1800-599-0019 (24/7 toll-free).
  • Vandrevala Foundation: Call +91 9999 666 555 (24/7 support).
