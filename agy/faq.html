Everything you need to know about online counselling across India, fees in ₹ INR, session formats in IST, and getting started.

Online counselling (teletherapy) connects you with a qualified mental health professional via secure, encrypted video or phone calls. Rather than traveling through city traffic to a physical clinic, you attend sessions from your home, private room, or any quiet setting where you feel comfortable.

Online sessions are available across India. Prior to your appointment, you receive a direct link. Clicking that link opens your private, password-protected virtual room in your browser—no complex software downloads required.

You need three simple things:

  • A smartphone, tablet, or laptop with a working camera and microphone.
  • A stable internet connection (broadband Wi-Fi or reliable 4G/5G mobile data).
  • A modern web browser such as Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge.

We also strongly recommend using earphones or headphones. This enhances audio clarity and ensures that only you hear your counsellor's voice, protecting your acoustic privacy at home.

Yes. Online sessions are accessible across India—whether you are in metropolitan cities like Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, or tier-2 and tier-3 towns.

All session timings are scheduled in Indian Standard Time (IST). Sessions can be conducted via video or audio-only phone calls depending on your preference and internet bandwidth.

Confidentiality is fundamental to our practice:

  • Video streams are protected with 256-bit encryption.
  • Sessions are never recorded or stored.
  • Administrative records are held in secure systems adhering to Indian data protection guidelines.

As with all professional psychological care, your discussions remain strictly confidential, subject only to mandatory legal exceptions (such as immediate risk of harm to yourself or others).

Insurance Notice in India: While most outpatient counselling in India is private-pay (via UPI, net banking, or debit/credit card), some comprehensive private health insurance policies have started covering teleconsultations and psychiatric care under the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 provisions. We provide itemised GST-compliant receipts that you can submit for reimbursement.

*Note: Final fees and sliding-scale arrangements must be verified and set by the organisation before launch.

We ask for at least 24 hours' notice in IST if you need to cancel or reschedule your session. Because your therapist reserves that dedicated slot solely for you, cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice may be subject to a late cancellation fee, barring unforeseen medical emergencies.

You can easily request a reschedule by emailing hello@mindfulspace.in or contacting your counsellor directly.

We recommend reviewing our About Us and Services pages to explore practitioner focus areas. When you submit your booking inquiry, you can note preferences such as language or focus.

Therapeutic rapport is essential. If after your initial session you feel that another counsellor's approach would suit you better, you can switch seamlessly without any hesitation or awkwardness.

Mindful Space is not an emergency crisis intervention service.

If you are in immediate danger or may harm yourself, contact your nearest hospital emergency department or local emergency services, and reach out to a trusted person who can stay with you.

  • Hospital Casualty: Visit the emergency department of your nearest hospital.
  • National Emergency Number: Dial 112 across India (verify local availability).
  • Tele-MANAS (Govt. of India): 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (24/7 toll-free).
  • KIRAN Helpline: 1800-599-0019 (24/7 toll-free).
  • Vandrevala Foundation: +91 9999 666 555 (24/7 support).
