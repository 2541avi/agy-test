1. Nature of This Demonstration Service

The website located at this domain is provided strictly as a demonstration interface for an online counselling service designed for individuals, couples, and students across India. All interactive forms, including session requests and newsletter signups, are frontend prototypes designed to test user interaction flows. No actual clinical, psychological, or counselling services are contracted or rendered through this prototype.

2. Critical Non-Emergency Disclaimer

Mindful Space provides non-emergency virtual counselling. It is not a replacement for emergency psychiatric care or hospital casualty facilities and is not intended for crisis intervention.

If you or someone in your proximity is in immediate danger or may harm themselves, contact your nearest hospital emergency department or local emergency services (such as 112 across India, subject to local availability), or connect with a 24/7 helpline such as Tele-MANAS (14416 / 1800-891-4416) or KIRAN (1800-599-0019), and reach out to a trusted person who can stay with you.

3. Informational Content & Non-Medical Advice

All editorial articles, journal prompts, and mental wellness resources published on this website are provided for general educational purposes. They do not constitute formal psychiatric evaluations, medical diagnoses, or individualised clinical treatment plans. Reading articles on this website does not establish a therapist-client relationship.

4. Eligibility & Age of Majority in India

In a live deployment, services would only be offered to individuals who have attained the age of majority (18 years or older under the Indian Majority Act, 1875), or to minors where verifiable written consent has been provided by a parent or legal guardian in accordance with Indian healthcare regulations.

5. Informed Consent & Telehealth Practice Guidelines

Prior to the commencement of live therapeutic sessions, formal informed consent documentation must be executed in alignment with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) / National Medical Commission (NMC). This includes:

  • Technological Considerations: Understanding potential connectivity disruptions and setting up acoustic privacy at home.
  • Confidentiality Boundaries: Clarification of mandated reporting requirements under Indian law (e.g., imminent physical self-harm, harm to others, or child protection statutes).
  • Voluntary Engagement: The absolute right of the client to discontinue counselling at any stage.

6. Fee Structure, Cancellations, and IST Scheduling

Standard terms for virtual practices in India require fees to be denominated in Indian Rupees (₹ INR). Clients are expected to provide at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice based on Indian Standard Time (IST) for rescheduling or cancellations to respect the therapist's reserved clinical hour.

7. Verification Prior to Commercial Launch

Counsellor qualifications, registration details (such as Rehabilitation Council of India / RCI credentials where applicable), fees, cancellation policies, and availability must be independently verified and replaced with the organisation's actual business information before launch.

8. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

Any formal dispute arising from a live version of this service would be governed by the laws of India, subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in India (placeholder: courts of New Delhi, Bengaluru, or Mumbai, India).
