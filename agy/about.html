Who We Are

Compassionate Care Grounded in Human Connection

A warm, thoughtful virtual counselling practice available across India, designed to support you at your own pace in Indian Standard Time (IST).

Our Mission

At Mindful Space, our mission is to make evidence-informed, deeply compassionate mental health counselling accessible, confidential, and destigmatised for individuals, couples, and students across India.

Navigating life's emotional terrain can be challenging—whether you are dealing with chronic stress, family and relationship dynamics, academic pressure, or career burnout. Finding trusted, empathetic support should never feel like an intimidating obstacle.

We established Mindful Space to bridge the gap between clinical excellence and genuine human warmth. By providing online teletherapy across metropolitan cities, tier-2, and tier-3 towns, we meet you right where you feel safest, removing travel stress, traffic commutes, and geographical barriers.

The Heart of Our Practice

"Healing does not occur on a rigid timeline. We honour your personal pace, celebrate your progress, and partner with you as you rediscover emotional clarity and self-compassion."

Guiding Principles

Our Core Values

Every interaction at Mindful Space is built upon four foundational pillars of care.

Radical Compassion

We meet you with empathy, deep listening, and zero judgement, creating a space where all feelings and backgrounds are valued.

Psychological Safety

Your confidentiality and privacy are paramount. We cultivate an inclusive, culturally sensitive, and affirmative space for all.

Evidence-Informed Care

Our practices draw from clinically grounded modalities, including CBT, mindfulness-based therapy, and somatic grounding tools.

Collaborative Autonomy

You are the foremost expert on your lived experience. We partner with you collaboratively without imposing rigid expectations.

Clinical Philosophy

Our Counselling Approach

We combine contemporary psychological science with contemplative mindfulness to support your mental and emotional wellbeing.

Person-Centred Care

Rooted in unconditional positive regard, we prioritise building a trusting therapeutic rapport where you feel genuinely understood, heard, and respected.

Core focus: Empathy, reflective dialogue, personal meaning-making, and emotional processing.

Cognitive & Mindfulness Integration

We blend Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) with Mindfulness-Based Cognitive Therapy (MBCT) and Acceptance and Commitment approaches.

Core focus: Identifying unhelpful thought cycles, emotional regulation, and nervous-system calming tools.

Trauma-Informed & Paced

We recognise that everyone carries unique life experiences. We move at your pace, fostering stability before delving into deeper exploration.

Core focus: Emotional safety, somatic regulation, boundary work, and grounding rituals.
