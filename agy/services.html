Individual Counselling • Adults (18+)

Individual Counselling

A private, one-on-one sanctuary to untangle anxiety, chronic stress, self-doubt, and emotional fatigue.

In individual counselling, you work closely with your therapist to explore thought patterns, understand difficult emotions, and establish sustainable coping strategies. Whether you are dealing with workplace pressure, boundary fatigue, or navigating personal transitions, we offer a safe, confidential space accessible anywhere in India.

Suitable Audience: Adults experiencing general anxiety, low mood, imposter feelings, relationship stress, or a desire for deeper self-awareness.
What to Expect: 50-minute virtual sessions held weekly or fortnightly via encrypted video or phone, scheduled in Indian Standard Time (IST), integrating CBT exercises, somatic grounding, and reflective self-compassion tools.

Common Themes Explored

  • • Persistent worry, overthinking, and somatic tension
  • • Workplace burnout and relentless inner self-criticism
  • • Unprocessed grief, emotional loss, or personal crossroads
  • • Setting healthy boundaries with family, colleagues, and friends
  • • Cultivating self-worth and mindful emotional presence
Partnerships & Relationships

Couples Counselling

Nurturing emotional safety, mutual understanding, and respectful communication between partners.

Relationships often encounter friction when unspoken needs, defensive habits, or busy professional schedules disrupt emotional intimacy. Our couples sessions provide a balanced, neutral environment where both partners feel equally heard, valued, and empowered to express themselves without blame.

Suitable Audience: Partners in any stage of relationship experiencing communication deadlocks, trust ruptures, major life changes, or a mutual wish to deepen emotional connection.
What to Expect: 60-minute joint video sessions in IST focused on identifying recurring conflict triggers, practising active listening in real-time, and co-creating shared relationship rituals.

Couples Focus Areas

  • • De-escalating recurring arguments and emotional shutdowns
  • • Restoring transparency and emotional vulnerability
  • • Navigating family dynamics and dual-career pressures
  • • Balancing parenting stress with relationship care
Students & Emerging Adults • Ages 18–26

Student & Young Adult Support

Empathetic guidance for the unique stressors of college life, competitive examinations, and early career choices.

The transition from school to higher education and the professional world can be disorienting. Rigorous academic expectations, competitive examinations, financial concerns, and living away from home often collide. We offer compassionate, confidential support tailored to student schedules across India.

Suitable Audience: Undergraduate and postgraduate students, entrance exam aspirants, and young professionals navigating independence, academic burnout, social isolation, or career uncertainty.
What to Expect: Flexible 50-minute virtual appointments in IST that fit around lecture timetables and exam periods, with practical focus on stress reduction, emotional regulation, and healthy study habits.

Key Topics Addressed

  • • Hostel adjustment, loneliness, and campus social pressures
  • • Balancing family expectations with personal aspirations
  • • Building healthy daily routines, screen boundaries, and sleep habits
Major Life Shifts & Adjustments

Stress & Life Transitions

Anchor yourself when familiar routines and life circumstances change.

Change—even positive change—can trigger intense stress, loss of identity, and disorientation. Whether you are stepping into a new job, relocating to another city across India, entering parenthood, or coping with retirement, we help you find steady footing and renewed perspective.

Suitable Audience: Individuals facing pivotal career shifts, city relocations, family adjustments, or major personal re-evaluations.
What to Expect: Supportive, practical sessions combining cognitive reframing, values assessment, nervous-system soothing, and actionable adjustment plans.

Logistics & Practicalities

What to Expect in Virtual Counselling

Practical information to help you feel completely comfortable prior to your first session.

1. Secure Session Link

You will receive a confidential, encrypted video room link by email 24 hours prior to your scheduled time in Indian Standard Time (IST). No software downloads are required.

2. Creating Your Space

Choose a private, quiet space where you will not be overheard or interrupted. Feel free to have a cup of warm tea, headphones, and a notepad handy.

3. At Your Own Speed

There is never any pressure to disclose everything in session one. We begin with what you feel comfortable sharing and proceed collaboratively.
