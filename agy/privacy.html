Legal & Privacy Notice: India Demonstration Prototype

This website is an illustrative design prototype built to demonstrate the frontend interface for an India-based online counselling platform. This document is a conceptual placeholder and does not constitute a legally binding privacy policy.

Mindful Space does not claim certified legal compliance. Prior to collecting real user information, storing intake forms, or delivering counselling services in India, the final legal and privacy wording must be reviewed and certified by qualified legal counsel in India for compliance with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules, the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017, and relevant Telemedicine Practice Guidelines.

Document Status: Prototype Draft / Educational Template • Jurisdiction: India • Effective Date: Demo Model (Not Live)

1. Purpose of This Prototype

This website serves as a functional demonstration for an online counselling service tailored to people across India. The interactive forms (newsletter signup and session booking inquiry) execute exclusively on the client-side inside your browser. No backend databases are connected, and no personal or health data is stored or transmitted to external servers.

2. Data Collection Blueprint for an India-Based Service

In a live, operational version of Mindful Space operating in India, the collection of user information would be governed by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023) and would typically cover:

  • Identity & Contact Information: Full name, verified email address, and Indian phone number (`+91 98XXX XXXXX`) for appointment coordination in Indian Standard Time (IST).
  • Non-Clinical Intake Preferences: Preferred counselling category (Individual, Couples, Student, Transitions), session format (Online Video, Phone, In-person), and language preferences.
  • Billing & Invoicing: Payment processing handled through Reserve Bank of India (RBI)-authorized payment gateways supporting UPI, Net Banking, and cards. Raw card credentials would never be stored on practice servers.
  • Technical Logs: IP addresses and browser headers strictly necessary for diagnostic performance and cybersecurity defense.

3. Teletherapy Privacy & Practice Standards in India

Prior to conducting live virtual sessions, technical and clinical safeguards must align with Indian regulatory frameworks:

  • Encrypted Communications: Video and audio sessions must utilize 256-bit encryption.
  • No Session Recordings: Consultations must never be recorded or transcribed without explicit written clinical consent.
  • Telemedicine Guidelines: Practice protocols must align with the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines issued by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) / National Medical Commission (NMC).

4. Mental Healthcare Act 2017 & Confidentiality Exceptions

In India, professional counselling notes and communications are protected by strict ethical confidentiality under the Mental Healthcare Act, 2017. However, mental health practitioners have legal and ethical obligations to breach confidentiality under specific statutory exceptions:

  1. Where there is an imminent threat of severe physical harm or danger to the client or another individual.
  2. Mandatory reporting requirements under Indian law, such as the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act.
  3. Compliance with a legally binding summons or order issued by an Indian court of competent jurisdiction.

5. Rights of Data Principals (DPDPA 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), users in India possess clear rights as Data Principals, including the right to access summaries of personal data, request correction or completion of inaccurate information, request erasure of data subject to clinical retention laws, and register grievances with a designated Data Protection Officer.

6. Verification Prior to Launch

Before commercial launch, all counsellor qualifications, registration details (such as Rehabilitation Council of India / RCI registration where applicable), fees, cancellation policies, and practitioner availability must be independently verified and updated with the organisation's actual business information.

7. Legal Review Inquiries
