How Virtual Counselling Works

We believe getting quality mental health support should be intuitive, transparent, and completely free of friction anywhere in India.

1

Find your support

Share your scheduling preferences in Indian Standard Time (IST) and general focus areas through our simple request form. We match you with an experienced counsellor suited to your needs.

2

Meet online

Join your private, encrypted video or phone session from any quiet space of your choosing—your room, balcony, or comfortable corner. No commuting or clinic waiting rooms needed.

3

Take your next step

Collaborate with your counsellor week-by-week. Unpack challenges, discover practical coping tools, and build sustainable inner resilience at your pace.

About Mindful Space

A human-centred approach to emotional healing.

We founded Mindful Space on a foundational belief: mental health support across India should be accessible, empathetic, and free of stigma.

Traditional counselling can sometimes feel distant, rigid, or logistically exhausting to schedule amidst busy daily routines. By providing secure online sessions across India, we remove commuting stress and allow you to reflect in the environment where you feel most at ease.

"Therapy isn't about being 'fixed.' It's about being witnessed, understood, and supported as you reconnect with your innate capacity to heal."
